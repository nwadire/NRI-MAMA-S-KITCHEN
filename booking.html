Frequently Asked Questions

Find out more about our Nigerian and Igbo food, catering and cooking services.

What services does NRI MAMA'S KITCHEN offer?

We prepare Nigerian and Igbo dishes, take food orders, and provide catering and cooking services for events and other occasions. Contact us to discuss what you need.

Can I provide my own ingredients and ask NRI MAMA'S KITCHEN to cook?

Yes. Choose our cooking service when booking and tell us about the ingredients and cooking materials you will provide. The cooking service fee will be confirmed by our team.

Can NRI MAMA'S KITCHEN supply the ingredients and cook for me?

Yes. With full catering, we can source ingredients and prepare your selected dishes. Share your guest count, food choices and event details so we can discuss your requirements and provide a quotation.

Do you offer food delivery?

Delivery arrangements can be discussed when you place an order. You can also choose customer pickup in the booking form. Please contact us to confirm delivery availability and any applicable arrangements.

Can I customize my food order?

Yes. Include your preferred dishes, additional proteins, combinations, cooking style, dietary requirements and other instructions in the order form. We will review your requests with you.

How do I book catering services?

Choose a service above, share your event details and food choices, then submit your prepared message to us on WhatsApp to complete your booking request.

How can I get a quotation for my event?

Send us your event type, date, location, number of guests and food selections through the booking form. We will review your requirements and confirm the applicable quotation. Cooking service fees are also confirmed by the business.
