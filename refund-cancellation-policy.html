Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Submitting a website form creates an enquiry; it does not by itself confirm an order or collect payment. Order details, availability, preparation timing and any cancellation terms should be confirmed directly with NRI MAMA'S KITCHEN.

Requesting a change or cancellation

Contact us as soon as possible by replying in the WhatsApp conversation used for the order, or call +234 816 205 0512. Provide your name, order or event details and the change requested. We will confirm whether the change or cancellation can be accommodated, taking account of food already prepared, ingredients already purchased, timing and the agreed service terms.

Refund requests

This website does not take payment and cannot issue refunds. If you paid directly to the business under an arrangement made outside the website, contact us through the original agreed channel with the order and payment details (do not send full card numbers or security codes). We will review the request and explain the outcome and any applicable refund arrangements.

Nothing in this policy limits rights or remedies that apply under Nigerian consumer protection law, including the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018 where applicable. Where an order cannot be fulfilled or is materially different from what was agreed, contact us promptly so we can investigate and discuss an appropriate resolution.
