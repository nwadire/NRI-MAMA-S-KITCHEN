Payment Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This website does not accept or process online payments. There is no checkout, payment gateway or card-payment system on this website.

How an order enquiry works

Use the order form to prepare your request and send it to the kitchen through WhatsApp. The form may show menu prices or an estimated subtotal to help describe your request; a final quotation and order acceptance are confirmed directly with you. The website does not collect money, process transactions or send payment instructions automatically.

Keep payment information safe

Never enter or send card numbers, CVV/security codes, banking passwords, one-time passcodes or other payment credentials in website forms, order notes or WhatsApp messages. Any arrangements agreed directly with the business are outside this website. Verify the recipient and terms directly before making any payment, and do not rely on an unsolicited payment request.

Refunds and questions

This website cannot take payment or issue a refund. For an arrangement made directly with the business, contact us on WhatsApp at +234 816 205 0512 or by email at Kelechinwadire@gmail.com. See our Refund and Cancellation Policy.

No payment gateway or new payment feature has been added. This policy does not endorse a particular payment method.
