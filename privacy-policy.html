Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

NRI MAMA'S KITCHEN (“we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be handled when you browse this website, request a booking or contact us. It is intended to be read with our Data Protection Policy.

Information you provide

When you fill in an order or booking form, you may enter your name, telephone or WhatsApp number, email address, event details, delivery or service address, food choices and instructions. Please provide only information needed to respond to your request and do not enter card details, passwords or other highly sensitive information.

How the website handles order information

This website is a frontend and does not send order details to a website server. Forms may save draft details and local demo orders in browser storage on the device you are using. The browser's storage remains on that device unless cleared; the local demo dashboard is not a secure, shared business system and must not be used to hold real customer records.

When you choose WhatsApp, the website opens a message draft addressed to our business number. You must review and press Send in WhatsApp; the website does not send it automatically. If you choose email, your email application opens a draft for you to review and send. WhatsApp, email and their providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

Why information is used

Information you send to us may be used to respond to an enquiry, discuss availability and quotations, arrange requested food or catering services, communicate about an order and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell customer information or use this website to process online payments.

Cookies and browser storage

Our current website uses essential browser storage for saved form progress, local demo orders and your cookie preference. We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. See the Cookie Policy and Data Protection Policy. Open Cookie Settings to review or change your choice.

Sharing and international services

We do not sell your information. If you choose to send an enquiry through WhatsApp or email, the selected service provider receives the information in that message and may process it in or outside Nigeria according to its own terms. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect legal rights.

Retention and security

Form drafts and local demo data remain in the browser until removed by you or cleared by the browser. Messages sent to us may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the service relationship and meet legal obligations. Because this site has no server-side customer database or authenticated admin system, it cannot provide centralized backups, access controls or secure deletion from a business system. Do not use the demo dashboard for real customer data.

Your choices and rights

You may clear this site's stored data in your browser, choose not to submit information, or contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent to us. Applicable rights may also include objection, restriction, portability and withdrawal of consent, subject to law and the circumstances of processing. Contact the relevant service provider separately about information held in its systems.

Contact

For privacy requests or questions, email Kelechinwadire@gmail.com or call +234 816 205 0512. See the Data Protection Policy for request details.

This policy describes the current frontend website. It is not a certification of compliance or a substitute for legal advice; our practices and this notice should be reviewed as the business and applicable law evolve.
