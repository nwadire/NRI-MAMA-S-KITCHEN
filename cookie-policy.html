Cookie Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy describes cookies and similar browser storage used by the current NRI MAMA'S KITCHEN website.

What is used now

The current site does not use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking cookies. It uses browser local storage (which is similar to cookies in that it saves information in your browser, but is not itself a cookie) to retain order-form drafts, local demo order records and your cookie preference. Essential storage lets the order forms preserve progress on the device being used.

When you follow a WhatsApp or email link, those third-party services may use their own cookies and technologies. Their practices are governed by their own notices and settings. This site does not load their tracking tools merely by displaying its pages.

Your choices

The consent banner offers Accept All, Reject Non-Essential and Cookie Settings. Since no non-essential cookie category is currently active, the site only records your preference in local storage and does not start analytics or advertising tracking. Essential storage used by order forms remains necessary for those features.

Use Cookie Settings in the Privacy Policy page to review or change your choice. You may also clear site data through your browser controls. Clearing it can remove saved order drafts, local demo orders and your recorded preference. Browser settings can block storage, but some form features may then not work as expected.

Changes and contact

If we add optional cookies or tracking in the future, we will update this policy and the consent choices before enabling those technologies where consent is required. For questions, email Kelechinwadire@gmail.com.

Consent choices do not create or enable analytics functions that are not present on the website.
