Data protection and your rights

Data Protection Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

NRI MAMA'S KITCHEN aims to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable Nigerian data protection requirements, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (“NDPA”) where it applies. This notice explains the current website's limited data handling and how to make a request. It is not a representation that a technical compliance audit or certification has been completed.

Information and purposes

Order and booking forms may ask for contact details, service or event information, addresses, food selections and instructions. We use information you choose to send to respond to and manage your enquiry, arrange services, communicate with you and meet legal obligations. Do not send payment credentials or unrelated sensitive information.

Current website limitations

This site has no server-side customer database, authenticated admin dashboard or server-based order submission. Form progress and local demo orders may be stored in browser local storage on your device. The admin demo is not access-protected, data is not synchronized or backed up, and browser storage is not suitable for real customer records. When you send an enquiry through WhatsApp or email, that service receives the message and handles it under its own privacy terms.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law and any relevant exceptions, you may have rights to request access to personal data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, data portability, or withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). Rights depend on the circumstances and may not all apply to every request.

How to make a request

Email Kelechinwadire@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”. Describe the request and provide enough context for us to identify the relevant enquiry. Do not include passwords, full card numbers or security codes. We may ask for reasonable information to verify identity and protect other people; we will not request sensitive credentials. We will respond within applicable legal timeframes and explain if a request cannot be fulfilled in full.

You can clear order drafts and demo order records directly in the browser where they were created. We cannot delete a message from WhatsApp, your email provider or another recipient's system; contact that provider or recipient about its copy.

Retention and security

Browser data remains until cleared by you or your browser. Information sent to the business may be kept only as reasonably needed to handle the request, provide services and comply with legal obligations. The present static website does not provide centralized backup, account access control, breach alerts or secure server-side deletion; those controls require an appropriately configured hosting and backend service.

Contact and complaints

For data protection questions, contact Kelechinwadire@gmail.com or +234 816 205 0512. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the NDPC through its official channels. See also our Privacy Policy.

This policy is a practical website notice, not legal advice. Obtain a current legal and technical assessment before using the demo dashboard or processing customer data at scale.
