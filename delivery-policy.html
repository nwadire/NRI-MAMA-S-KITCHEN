Getting your order

Delivery Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Delivery is arranged directly with the kitchen and is subject to location, availability, order size and timing. The website does not calculate delivery coverage, confirm a delivery booking or dispatch a courier automatically.

Confirming an arrangement

Include a complete delivery address, contact number and useful directions in your WhatsApp enquiry. We will confirm whether delivery can be arranged, the expected time, any delivery charge and other details before accepting the arrangement. A requested time is not confirmed until we reply.

Pickup and receiving orders

Where available, pickup details will be agreed directly. Please make sure someone can receive the order at the confirmed address and time and tell us promptly about changes. Preparation or travel delays can occur; contact us through the agreed WhatsApp conversation if an order is late or the delivery details need correction.

Food quality and order concerns

Check the order when it arrives and contact us as soon as possible if an item is missing, damaged or materially different from what was confirmed. We will review the details and discuss an appropriate resolution, subject to applicable consumer rights.
